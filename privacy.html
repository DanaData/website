Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy explains how Danadata Ltd ("DanaData", "we") handles personal information on this website, danadata.net.

Using one of our products? Each has its own privacy policy, which applies when you use it:

  • DanaTel privacy policy
  • DataLion privacy policy

What we collect

Only what you send us. If you use the contact form or email us, we receive your name, email address and message. We don't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers on this website.

How we use it

To reply to you and handle your enquiry. We don't sell your information or add you to marketing lists.

How long we keep it

As long as needed to deal with your enquiry and keep reasonable business records, then we delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email info@danadata.net. If you're unhappy with our response, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
